Retain support.

Start here for clipboard capture, shortcuts, and settings.

Report a problem

Use our public GitHub issue tracker for bugs and feature requests. A GitHub account is required. Include your macOS version, Retain version/build from About Retain, and steps to reproduce.

Reports are public. Never include clipboard contents, passwords, tokens, personal information, or screenshots containing sensitive items. We will never ask for your clipboard history.

Report an issue

A private support email channel is being prepared. No public download is available during Beta preparation.

Common questions

How do I open Retain?

Click Retain in the menu bar, or press Control–Shift–V. Change the shortcut in Settings. If the icon is obscured by the camera notch, macOS may have too little menu-bar space; the shortcut remains another way to open Retain.

Why are new copies missing?

Check that capture is not paused. On macOS versions offering clipboard access controls, check Retain under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Paste from Other Apps. Unsupported data, file references, and content marked concealed, transient, or automatically generated are skipped.

How does selecting an item work?

Selection restores the item to your clipboard by default. Optional automatic paste attempts to paste into the app you used before opening Retain. It requires native event permission; without permission, paste manually. Retain does not request permission merely to run automated tests.

How do limits and pins work?

The recent-item limit excludes pinned items. Lowering the limit removes excess unpinned entries. Clear History removes unpinned entries. Delete individual pinned entries to remove them too.

Will updates reset my settings?

Retain saves preferences and history outside the app bundle. Updates are designed to preserve them. Reset Settings is a separate confirmed action; it restores defaults and can prune excess unpinned items. Real TestFlight update testing is still pending.

How do I remove stored content?

Delete individual entries, including pins, or clear unpinned history. Reset Settings does not clear all history. Removing the app can leave app data on your Mac. Contact support for data-removal help before deleting files manually.
