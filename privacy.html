Your clipboard, your Mac

Privacy policy.

Effective September 26, 2026. Applies to the current Retain macOS Beta implementation and this support website, provided by Zuriel Labs.

What Retain stores

While running and not paused, Retain checks for new clipboard copies and stores supported text and images locally on your Mac. History includes content, timestamps, duplicate-detection hashes, and pin state. Retain saves preferences such as your shortcut and recent-item limit. Copies can contain sensitive information; consider pausing capture before copying sensitive content.

What Retain skips

Retain skips file references and items marked concealed, transient, or automatically generated by the source app. These markers are an app convention, not a guarantee that every password or secret is excluded. Retain does not promise to detect all sensitive content.

Local storage and retention

Retain stores text in a local history file, images in local PNG files, and preferences through macOS UserDefaults. Retain does not add its own encryption to these files. Device security and any configured disk encryption remain your responsibility. Unpinned history is limited by your setting; pinned items remain until deleted. Pausing capture does not delete existing history.

Your controls

You can pause capture, change the recent-item limit, delete entries, unpin items, and clear unpinned history. Lowering the limit or confirming Reset Settings can remove excess unpinned entries. Reset Settings does not delete all history. Updates are designed to retain available settings and history; uninstalling the app may leave that data on your Mac. Deleted entries are removed from Retain's active storage; Retain cannot erase independent backups or guarantee forensic erasure.

Transmission and permissions

The current Retain app has no developer-operated server connection, cloud synchronization, account system, advertising, or analytics SDK. Zuriel Labs does not receive your clipboard history through Retain. Restoring an item writes it to the system clipboard, where other apps may access it under macOS controls. Optional automatic paste delivers selected content to the previously active app; that app's privacy practices apply. Clipboard access, event posting, and Launch at Login follow macOS controls and your choices.

Website and support

This static site has no added analytics, advertising, cookies, forms, or embedded third-party assets. GitHub hosts the site and may process technical request information, including IP addresses, under its own privacy statement. Clicking external links opens services governed by their own policies.

Support currently uses public GitHub issues. Your report and GitHub identity may be publicly visible. Only share information needed to investigate your issue; never send clipboard history or secrets. Zuriel Labs uses information you voluntarily provide to respond and investigate. A private support email channel is not yet available.

Apple distribution

Future App Store and TestFlight distribution is subject to Apple's privacy practices, including information Apple processes for delivery and diagnostics. Availability is not claimed by this policy. Any additional collection introduced in Retain will require a policy update before release.

Questions and changes

Contact Zuriel Labs through the support page for questions about this policy. Do not post private information publicly. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
