Free clipboard history for macOS

Keep what you copy.

Text, images, and the things you need again. Retain keeps clipboard history close, in a compact menu-bar picker.

Explore RetainRead the privacy policy

Beta preparation. Retain is in development. Public TestFlight access and an App Store download are not available yet.

Copy. Find. Restore.

Search past text and images. Select an item to restore it to your clipboard, then paste it where you need it.

Keep your favorites.

Pinned items have their own section and stay outside your recent-item limit. New installations start with 25 recent items.

Make it yours.

Customize your shortcut, recent-item limit, long-text preview, and optional automatic paste. Pause capture for a set time or until you resume.

Stored on your Mac.

Retain stores history locally. No Retain account, cloud sync, or developer analytics. Review the privacy policy before storing sensitive content.
